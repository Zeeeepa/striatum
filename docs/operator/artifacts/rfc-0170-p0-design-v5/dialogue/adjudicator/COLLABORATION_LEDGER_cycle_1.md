`needs_revision` is reserved (per the gate) for: an unsound/non-mechanical predicate,

**safety** property, a G3/G4 regression, or a deferral that disguises a P0-blocking
defect. **None of these holds.** A deferred whole-tree-exactness item (#618) or a
non-cooperative-hang liveness item (#619) is **P1 by operator decision** and does not
