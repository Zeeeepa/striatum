# Evidence Audit

Read the synthesis, the support ledger, and the referenced evidence files.
For each ledger row, decide whether the claim is `supported`,
`partially_supported`, `unsupported`, `contradicted`, or `not_audited`, and
record the verdict in a `finding` artifact under
`docs/support-ledger-flow/audit/`. Do not modify the synthesis or the
support ledger; this job is review-only.
