# Record The Decision

Create `examples/implementation-panel-flow/artifacts/DECISION.md` with
`striatum.decision.v1` front matter:

```yaml
---
schema_version: striatum.decision.v1
artifact_kind: decision
decision_id: implementation-panel-flow-001
run_id: TBD
owner: human
outcome: accepted
follow_up_required: true
title: Implementation panel decision
created_at: 2026-05-22T00:00:00Z
---
```

Record the final decision in terms a maintainer can act on:

- Accepted implementation path.
- Rejected alternatives.
- Follow-up work and owners.
- Test and rollout gates.
- Conditions that should reopen the decision.
